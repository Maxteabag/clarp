## Provider policy and cache ownership

The queue keeps its existing provider policy, including no fallback after a
chunk reaches its sink. Raw PCM remains Cartesia-only. The engine names the
preserved one-off policy `LEGACY_ONE_OFF_LADDER` and the configured preview
policy `CONFIGURED_PREVIEW_LADDER`. Herald, ring openings and context retain
their legacy policy; extending it is the separate VP-10b product decision.

The signed VP-10 changes preview and controller narration fallback only.
Ordinary provider failures try the configured usable fallback. The Google ID
collision/unverifiable guards and `voice_unavailable` never trigger fallback.
If fallback fails, its failure is logged and the primary's original error and
status are returned, including when the fallback itself encounters a guard.

`synth_preview` retains the requested primary's original text, voice and
provider-specific preview options. Its per-call registry preserves ElevenLabs
preview transport/model/speed, custom adapter preview operations and generic
provider synthesis. Primary success is tracked by the provider wrapper, without
depending on logging. The existing primary cache keys and directories remain.
Substitute audio is served synchronously from a temporary file as `audio/mpeg`
and then removed; it is never cached under the primary voice's key. A later
request retries the primary. Worker/default text markup handling is unchanged.

## Regeneration and forward ports

The codec move is generated by S9's split tool from main's source at every
main-to-integration merge. The `s7-wav-to-mp3-import` repoint updates the custom
adapter import. Do not forward-port these generated files by hand.

The three S3 handlers are regenerated from main's pre-move `server/server.py`.
Their S7 edits are stored in
`tools/refactor/s3_replay/s7-3c-preview-narration.diff` with its ordered entry in
`tools/refactor/s3_replay.txt`. Stage A applies the replay patches before S3
extracts helpers and route groups. A direct edit to generated `routes/voice.py`
would be overwritten. Update the replay patch and prove a fresh `s3_merge`
reproduces the handlers with the identity gates. The handlers require the
integration-side `synth_preview` helper.

The captain's `logs/fwdport-check.py` and `logs/fwdport-watch.txt` stop a main
merge when watched hand-written code changes. Forward ports belong in the same
merge, with review and the required replay comparison:

- `transcription_pipeline.transcribe` or `_run_call_steps`: map a main change
  into the corresponding helper above, or `stt_router` for engine choice.
- Raw/chunked delivery lifecycle: update the shared file session or the
  delivery-specific subclass as appropriate.
- `transcript_streamer._on_text`: preserve its matching mode when updating
  `extract_speak_blocks`; preserve the other reader's mode too.
- `tts_worker._synthesize` / `_synth_pwa_via_delivery`, `tts_engine.py` and
  `tts_mode.py`: map provider/voice/credential changes to the registry,
  fallback/events/stream safety to the service, and delivery sequencing to the
  worker or engine. The step-3 watch is active.
- `tts_queue.recent_synth_count`: if main adds a caller, resolve that dependency
  before the merge rather than retaining the deletion blindly.
