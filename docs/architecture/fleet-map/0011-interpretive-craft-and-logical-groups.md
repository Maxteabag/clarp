# 0011 — Interpretive craft and logical groups

Status: Accepted. Explicit owner clarification, 2026-09-06.

## Context

The uniform workspace bubbles were a literal and unsatisfying interpretation of
an overall map. Peter wants an expressive, handcrafted view of meaningful work,
systems and relationships, not a file explorer dressed as bubbles. His examples
are explanations of his taste and reasoning, not exhaustive specifications.

## Decision

Optimize for understanding at a glance: who is doing what, how systems relate,
what changed, and where work or trouble is occurring. Choose logical groups and
visual emphasis that explain those questions. Filesystem containment is one
source of evidence, not a compulsory layout or the subject of the product.

Related working copies should feel like parts of one project where verified
relationships support that interpretation. Distinct projects, working copies,
services, artifacts and integrations need not share one shape or size. Size can
serve semantic significance, composition and useful detail. No universal rule
such as equal areas, bytes-to-area, commit-count-to-area or compulsory hierarchy
is accepted. This supersedes the equal-workspace-size interpretation of 0009;
selection still inspects without promoting one place or hiding the rest.

Give source authors room to invent compositions, grouping systems, local motion,
textures, transformations and interactions. Aim for deliberate craft rather than
identical containers with different labels. A branch analogy, an attached organic
lobe, a machine or a gear are illustrative ideas, not a required vocabulary.
Authors must make their own aesthetic decisions within this direction and explain
what information their choices communicate. Do not merely reproduce the examples.

Visible work should outlast a fleeting blink enough to be understood. Distinguish
recorded running actions from completed traces and old context. An empty-looking
map is a reason to inspect observation coverage and visual emphasis, never to
fabricate activity. Real services and integrations deserve representation when
observed. A shell command's overall success does not establish the success of
every operation inside a compound script.

## Continuity and freedom

Preserve recognizable identities, useful relationships, avatars, navigation,
truthful evidence and sandbox constraints. Compatible structural invention is
encouraged; a bland existing shape is not sacred. Ordinary novelty does not
justify replacing the entire world with an unrelated theme. The current explicit
request authorizes a meaningful composition change, not just more icons.

The first source implementation is a revisable design, not a new rulebook.
Judge it visually using real activity, readable close views, overview, phone
navigation and labeled demonstrations. Keep model authoring separate from frames.

## Authoring the viewed software

Flow becomes the active source-authoring baseline for this requested iteration.
Previously it was a static prototype while autonomous source changes affected
World only. Retain the previous World source by view identity in the JSON library;
a Flow publication must not overwrite it or appear under the World tab. The
observer and author use the active program, and compatible edits keep its view
identity. Selecting a UI tab does not itself change the authoring target.
