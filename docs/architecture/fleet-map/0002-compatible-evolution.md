# 0002 — Creative expansion must preserve established concepts

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-09-06  
Supersedes: The earlier interpretation that routine novelty authorizes whole-world reinvention.

## Context

Astra changed The Lantern Works' boats and workshops into a moth-and-loom world
after observing additional paths, a checkout and actions. No evidence required
boats to become moths. The prompt had conflated creative freedom with a request
to redesign everything. Peter rejected that disruption and clarified the balance.

## Decision

Preserve the general concepts and expand in the direction the application is
already going. Prefer existing representations, targeted repairs and compatible
extensions. A new agent, filename, checkout or repeated action usually requires
no source change. Meaningful novel behavior can receive a creative new mechanism
that belongs in the current world.

A structural rewrite requires a strong, concrete demand, such as an explicit
owner request or a demonstrated incompatibility that cannot be solved locally.
Ordinary novelty, an “Improve this detail” click, a model-generated idea, and a
revision increment do not authorize an aesthetic reset. Broad disruptive changes
are separate from ordinary automatic evolution.

## Consequences

Ordinary authors return scoped source-fragment edits or an unchanged result,
with the evidence for the change and an explanation of what remains intact.
Whole-program or whole-file replacement is rejected in the ordinary update path.
The program title, entry point, unrelated modules and established visual meanings
remain stable. Routine compatible work still applies without a review queue.
These mechanical checks support the intent; they do not prove semantic continuity
by themselves. Inspect the result and correct deviations.
