02 / Three things to make obvious

Movement, waiting,
and memory.

Follow the same work

Let a recognizable seal connect edits, a review request, checks and an actual delivery. Preserve the distinction between “mentioned,” “assigned,” “accepted” and “delivered.”

A request to review does not prove the reviewer started. A push does not prove a service deployed.

Show a real blockage

Where a recorded dependency exists, show work gathering or pausing at the relevant boundary. Help the viewer see what is waiting on what, without reading a queue transcript.

Silence, an old timestamp or a quiet avatar is not evidence of a blocker.

Leave a useful trace

Past observed interactions can leave restrained routes. Familiar patterns become recognizable even when there is little live activity.

Repeated contact is a pattern, not proof of causality or a required workflow.

04 / Proposed assignment for Mappy

Four tasks.
One connected story.

My recommendation is a focused pilot: one workflow that actually crosses meaningful boundaries. Extend its evidence support where necessary, then judge the whole scene.

01

Find the real route

Inspect the available task, message, validation, artifact and deployment records. Choose a workflow with enough explicit links to explain a journey.

Deliver: an evidence map separating observed relationships, time-based attribution and missing links. If a required relation is not recorded, identify the smallest useful instrumentation change.

02

Make the journey legible

Implement coherent motion and identity across the chosen route. Make belonging, a request, actual collaboration and delivery distinguishable.

Deliver: one end-to-end interaction you can follow at ordinary zoom. Choose your own materials and mechanisms; this diagram is not a prescribed rail network.

03

Give waiting and history a form

Show an evidenced dependency or interruption at its actual boundary. Let past interactions settle into restrained, distinguishable traces.

Deliver: a scene where live motion, unresolved waiting and historical routes cannot be confused. Keep noise bounded as activity grows.

04

Prove the full picture

Replay real activity with labels hidden. Inspect busy and quiet moments, phone and desktop, unknown evidence, and at least one counterexample.

Deliver: tested implementation, screenshots and animation proof, updated authoring guidance and a report to Nadia. Integrate only after preserving any newer learned source.

05 / The bar for success

Can you read the workflow
without reconstructing it?

  1. I can follow one piece of work through the systems it actually touched.
  2. I can distinguish a relationship from an action happening now.
  3. I can see a recorded blocker and what would release it.
  4. I can distinguish today’s motion from the traces of earlier work.
  5. The overview stays readable as more agents participate.
  6. Uncertain or missing evidence does not become confident animation.

Stage three is not yet assigned. This proposal does not promise a new data source is already available or replace the whole visual theme.
