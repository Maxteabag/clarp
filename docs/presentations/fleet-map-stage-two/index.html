02 / A language you can feel

Different meanings deserve
different behavior.

Belonging

Parts of one project share material and grow together. The relationship stays still when no work is happening.

Delivery

On an evidenced transfer, a recognizable object moves along a route. A configured remote alone never sends a package.

Discovery

Consulted sources gather around the work. A published report can gather those fragments into one outcome.

For this stage, choose the smallest vocabulary that makes one real workflow clear. Collaboration requires actual message evidence; a plan reference is not automatically a handoff.

03 / Less decoding, more recognition

The object should earn
its space on the map.

At overview scale

Recognize who is working, where attention is needed, and which objects changed. Quiet detail settles back.

When you move closer

Reveal the real preview, useful name, and local interactions. Inspection supplies provenance and uncertainty.

Proposed zoom-aware detail. Zoom changes what is legible; selecting an object does not enlarge its project or shrink everyone else.

04 / The assignment I’d give Mappy

One convincing workflow.
Four delivery tasks.

Start from the integrated stage-one baseline. Own the artistic decisions and ship a coherent visual slice before extending it to every entity.

01

Find the story

Choose one real workflow with recorded edits, validation, a recoverable interruption and a published artifact. Identify what the viewer should notice.

Deliver: an evidence storyboard and a clear account of what is known, attributed or missing. Do not invent intermediate progress.

02

Give it a material

Develop an expressive object that keeps its identity as work changes it. Explore a few silhouettes or treatments, then choose and implement the strongest.

Deliver: one distinctive source implementation with readable interruption, recovery and outcome. The demo above is inspiration, not a required design.

03

Choreograph the scene

Tune the agent, object and relationships together. Make delivery distinct from ownership. Let completed work settle; reserve motion for information.

Deliver: a composition that reads at ordinary zoom and on a phone, with useful detail appearing as you approach. Preserve pan, pinch and reduced motion.

04

Prove it without captions

Watch the real replay with action labels hidden. Inspect screenshots and video. Fix ambiguity and overlap, then document the vocabulary for future authors.

Deliver: working code, visual evidence, appropriate regression checks and a report to Nadia for integration. No reset of the learned live source.

05 / What makes stage two successful?

A picture that explains
what happened.

We’re evaluating comprehension and visual craft, not how many animations were added.

The evidence plumbing from stage one remains the foundation. This stage concentrates on expression, composition and readability. A broad theme replacement is outside this proposal.

  1. I can follow the same piece of work across stages.
  2. I can distinguish work in progress, interruption, recovery and publication.
  3. I can recognize the real result when a preview exists.
  4. I can read the overview without opening every card.
  5. I can explore on a phone without losing my place.
  6. The picture admits uncertainty instead of inventing a story.

06 / Your direction, if you want to add it

Leave room for taste.

My recommendation: give Mappy this outcome and let him choose the visual treatment. Judge the result by what it communicates, rather than specifying every shape.
